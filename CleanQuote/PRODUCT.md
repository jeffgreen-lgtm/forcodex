# PRODUCT.md — CleanQuote

## One sentence
CleanQuote helps solo residential cleaning professionals price a job consistently, understand their effective hourly rate, and send a professional quote in under a minute.

## Target customer
Primary: solo residential house cleaner, owner-operator cleaning business, new/growing cleaner who does their own quoting. Secondary: very small cleaning company where the owner still prepares estimates.

Not targeted in V1: large janitorial companies, commercial facilities teams, franchises, multi-crew dispatch operations.

## Customer problem
Many cleaners price inconsistently because they mentally combine square footage, bedrooms, bathrooms, cleaning type, condition, add-ons, recurring frequency, expected labor time, and desired hourly earnings. The result can be underpricing, awkward quote messages, and uncertainty about profitability.

## Product promise
> Price confidently. Quote consistently.

CleanQuote does not claim to know the “correct market price.” It applies the cleaner’s own business rules consistently.

## Product principles
1. Their price, not ours.
2. Math before AI.
3. Fast enough for a text inquiry.
4. Profit visibility.
5. Local-first.
6. Professional output.
7. Small by design.

## Core loop
New inquiry → enter property/job → calculate → review profitability → save → share quote.

## Business model
Free: full onboarding/pricing setup and up to 3 quote creations.

Pro: unlimited quotes and PDF quote sharing.

Initial planned pricing: $8.99/month or $59.99/year. Do not hard-code production StoreKit identifiers until configured.

## Positioning
CleanQuote is a specialized pricing utility, not a generic AI estimator, invoice platform, scheduling app, marketplace, or bookkeeping product.

## Desired personality
Professional, warm, calm, straightforward. Avoid corporate jargon, fake AI-magic language, aggressive hustle culture, or implying prices are market guarantees.

## Core value statement
“If one accurate quote prevents one underpriced job, CleanQuote can pay for itself.”
