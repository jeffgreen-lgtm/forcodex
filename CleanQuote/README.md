**Product:** CleanQuote  
**Platform:** iPhone / iOS  
**Implementation:** SwiftUI + SwiftData + StoreKit 2  
**MVP principle:** Estimate → Quote → Send  
**Business principle:** Near-zero recurring infrastructure cost.

## MVP success definition

A solo residential cleaner can configure how they price jobs, enter a prospective cleaning job in under a minute, receive a deterministic recommended quote and estimated labor time, see the effective hourly rate, save the customer and quote locally, share a professional quote message, generate/share a simple PDF quote, use three quotes free, and unlock unlimited quotes with CleanQuote Pro.

The app must not require an account, server, API key, external database, or AI service.
