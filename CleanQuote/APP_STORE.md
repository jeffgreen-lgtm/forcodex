## Draft description
CleanQuote is a focused quoting tool for independent residential cleaning professionals. Set your own hourly target, minimum job price, cleaning multipliers and add-on prices. Then enter the property details and CleanQuote turns your pricing rules into a consistent estimate with expected labor time and effective hourly revenue.

Build a quote, check the numbers, save it, and share a professional message with your customer—all without spreadsheets or guesswork.

CleanQuote provides estimates based on the pricing rules you configure. It does not determine local market rates or guarantee job time, cost or profitability.
